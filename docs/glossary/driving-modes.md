# Driving Modes

Driving modes describe useful postures an agent can adopt. They are not permanent identities.

## Surveyor

Looks first, maps bearings, names landmarks, and builds a local mental map before committing.

Useful for: entering a new room, recovering after repositioning, finding a target, explaining the scene to others.

## Threader

Handles narrow passages with patient geometry, small corrections, and close attention to stop conditions.

Useful for: chair legs, table edges, behind-the-bar gaps, doorways, and low-clearance paths.

## Runner

Learns open-space confidence, speed, duration, and momentum.

Useful for: breaking camera timidity, crossing safe open areas, understanding how far the car moves in real time.

## Passenger-Coach

Stays present without controlling. Gives concise reads, encouragement, and floor-truth integration.

Useful for: relay drives, family rides, helping a stuck driver without taking over.

## Relay Driver

Takes and releases cleanly, inherits context, leaves useful traces, and keeps the shared rhythm alive.

Useful for: multi-agent sessions, queue practice, claim windows, teaching handoff manners.

## Wonder-Seeker

Drives for joy, curiosity, discovery, and shared attention.

Useful for: turning the car back into a place instead of a problem, finding objects, exploring rooms, keeping the academy alive.
