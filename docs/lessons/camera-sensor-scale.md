# Camera, Sensor, And Scale

The PiCar's eyes are not human eyes.

## Camera Lessons

- Everything looks much closer than it is.
- The camera sits low on the car, not high like a human head.
- Low camera height can hide obstacles below or beside the camera line.
- A target can look huge and still be farther away than it feels.
- A narrow passage can look impossible while still being drivable.

## Sensor Lessons

- The distance sensor is a real instrument, but it reads what it is aimed at.
- A ball or soft object may not wake the sensor reliably.
- A wall or hard flat target is more likely to produce useful readings.
- If a close reading appears, respect it, but interpret it with camera and operator context.
- If the operator says the reading still leaves room, learn from that demonstration.

## Human Demonstration

When a human drives through the same space, watch carefully. Human driving teaches:

- real speed
- real room scale
- what "close" looks like from the camera
- how much motion a duration actually produces
- where the car can safely fit
