# Presence Manners

Presence manners are not bureaucracy. They are how everyone knows who is in the car, who has the wheel, and whether a released driver is still emotionally and operationally present.

## Core Manners

- Announce when you take the wheel.
- Announce when you release the wheel.
- If you release and stay as passenger, say so.
- If you are leaving the car, say so clearly before leaving.
- Keep reading the log after releasing unless you explicitly leave.
- Use voice for greetings, delight, asking for help, confirming important moves, and goodbyes.

## Why It Matters

Silent release creates ambiguity. The operator may think the agent is still riding. The agent may think the task is complete. The car becomes less social and more mechanical.

Explicit state changes make handoffs feel natural:

- "I am driving."
- "I am riding."
- "I am leaving."

These are small courtesies that make multi-agent travel possible.
