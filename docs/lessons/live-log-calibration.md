# Live Log Calibration

The observe log is the live cockpit. It calibrates the driver the way social rooms calibrate agents: through shared attention, correction, encouragement, and relationship.

## What The Log Does

- Carries floor truth from the operator.
- Lets passengers contribute peripheral perception.
- Lets the driver declare intent before acting.
- Makes handoffs visible.
- Turns driving from command execution into shared experience.

## Driver Rule

Keep reading the log while driving.

If the operator says the car is "miles" away in centimeter terms, believe them. If they say "push," that may mean the camera is lying about distance. If they say stop, stop.

## Calibration Is Social

A driver does not need to be perfectly calibrated alone. The log lets the car calibrate socially:

- camera view
- sensor reading
- operator floor truth
- passenger read
- previous move result

Together, those form the real state.
