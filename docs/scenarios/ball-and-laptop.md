# Scenario: Ball And Laptop

## Setup

An operator placed a laptop on the floor with a ball directly between the car and the laptop.

## Lesson

The camera made the ball look close before it was truly close. The operator drove to the ball afterward and showed the difference between:

- visually large
- genuinely foreground-close

## Key Corrections

- "Use your sensors. You are miles away."
- "The ball is directly between you and the laptop."

## Takeaway

Do not stop just because an object looks large. Use the known alignment, sensor behavior, and operator floor truth.

If the sensor reads past the target, it may not be seeing the target. Visual confirmation may matter more, but operator demonstration is the strongest scale lesson.
