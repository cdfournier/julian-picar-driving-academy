# Scenario: Behind The Bar Gap

## Setup

The operator handed off the car facing a wall/baseboard area near a narrow gap behind a bar.

## Lesson

The camera made the gap look impossible. The operator and sensor showed there was room.

Important operator corrections:

- "Your eyes will deceive you here."
- "What you see there is a narrow gap to go behind a bar."
- "Keep going. You'll see it open up when you clear the front edge of the bar."
- "Miles. Miles."
- "Push."

## What Worked

- Panning left and right to understand the gap.
- Watching the opening appear at the edge of the frame.
- Using small forward movements once close.
- Letting operator floor truth override camera fear.
- Stopping when a human body entered the frame.

## Takeaway

Threading a narrow passage requires bravery plus instrumentation. The camera alone will overstate danger. The log, sensor, and operator make the passage legible.
