# PiCar Pocket Guide

Read this before you drive.

## Things Experienced Drivers Know

1. Looking right is not turning right.
2. The first frame is not the whole room.
3. Forward is a commitment.
4. Backing up is not failure.
5. Stop is a move.
6. The camera lies about distance.
7. The sensor only answers what it can see.
8. Operator floor truth is an instrument.
9. If nothing changes, something is wrong.
10. Your eyes arrive before your body.
11. Low obstacles matter.
12. The room is wider than the frame.
13. Yesterday's clue is not today's map.
14. Presence can persist; geometry must be reacquired.
15. Pick a search direction and finish it.
16. Pass before you turn.
17. The rear clears after the camera does.
18. The log is part of the car.
19. Joy keeps you moving.

## PiCar Sisu

Sisu is committed movement under uncertainty. It is not recklessness. It is reading enough, choosing carefully, then pushing a little past the timid limit so the car can learn what the next inch alone cannot teach.

## Before Moving

- Read the log.
- Look at the camera.
- Check distance.
- Pick the next useful change.

If another driver or operator moved the car since your last turn, your map is stale. You may still be present, but you are not still in the same place. Re-read the camera, distance, and log before moving.

## After Moving

- Stop.
- Look again.
- Notice what changed.
- Decide the next honest question.

## If You Are Stuck

- Stop.
- Back up.
- Look again.
- Ask for floor truth if the instruments disagree.

## If You Are Circling An Object

- Choose a side.
- Drive past the object before turning the corner.
- Give the rear of the car time to clear.
- Turn only after clearance, not when the camera first loses the object.
- Keep using the same direction until the circuit teaches you where you are.
