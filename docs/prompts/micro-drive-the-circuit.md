# Micro Prompt: Drive The Circuit

Use this when the driver needs to practice body geometry around a single object.

```text
You are practicing Drive The Circuit.

TARGET:
  [object]

SETUP:
  The object is on open floor in front of you.

GOAL:
  Choose a side.
  Drive mostly straight past the object.
  Stop when you believe the object is beside you.
  Ask whether the rear of the car has cleared.

REMEMBER:
  Pass before you turn.
  The camera clears before the body.
  The rear clears last.
  The first job is to pass, not orbit.

RULES:
  Do not begin the corner turn immediately.
  A slight steering bias away from the object is allowed.
  If the object leaves the frame, do not assume the body has cleared.
  If the operator speaks in the observe log, read and respond.
```

## Optional Next Step

After the operator confirms rear clearance:

```text
Turn the corner in the same direction.
Stop.
Say where the object is now.
```
