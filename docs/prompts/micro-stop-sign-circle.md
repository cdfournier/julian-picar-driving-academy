# Micro Prompt: STOP Sign Circle

Use this when the driver needs to practice repeatable turning and target acquisition.

```text
You are practicing STOP Sign Circle.

SETUP:
  Four STOP signs are placed around the car.
  They are not perfectly aligned.

GOAL:
  Keep turning in one direction.
  Find all four STOP signs.
  Count a STOP sign as acquired when it is readable.

REMEMBER:
  Use committed arcs.
  Readable is enough.
  Do not chase perfect centering.
  Small corrections change the path.
  If low geometry tightens, stop and recover.
```

## Optional Fewer-Moves Version

```text
Acquire all four STOP signs with fewer body moves than last time.
Use bold arcs to acquire.
Use small corrections only for safety or readability.
Boldness is not recklessness.
```
